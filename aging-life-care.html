Aging Life Care Professional®

What is Aging Life Care?

Aging Life Care™, also known as geriatric care management, is a holistic, client-centered approach to caring for older adults or others facing ongoing health challenges. Working with families, the expertise of Aging Life Care Professionals provides the answers at a time of uncertainty.

Our Professional Community

Part of a national association

Caring with Grace is part of a growing national organization, the Aging Life Care Association®. We learn from over 2,000 colleagues across the country, grow through professional development, and share a commitment to a strong code of ethics and standards of practice.

What We Do

Nine areas of life we help families navigate

Housing

Evaluate aging-in-place, residential care, assisted living, memory care.

Home Care Services

Source, screen, and supervise in-home caregivers when needed.

Medical Management

Attend appointments, manage medications, coordinate specialists.

Communication

Keep family members informed and aligned, wherever they live.

Social Activities

Connect clients to programs and communities that keep them engaged.

Legal Navigation

Coordinate with elder-law attorneys for advance directives, POAs, and more.

Financial Coordination

Work alongside financial advisors and family to plan for care costs.

Entitlements

Help navigate Medicare, Medicaid, VA benefits, and long-term care insurance.

Safety & Security

Home assessments, fall prevention, technology solutions for aging in place.
