Our Services

Care Management for Professionals

When your client needs more than financial, legal, or medical advice, Caring with Grace provides the professional care coordination and advocacy that families and clients need. We work alongside the existing professional team as a trusted partner.

Who we partner with

Financial & Wealth Advisors Trust Officers & Fiduciaries Legal Professionals Medical Professionals Home Care Agencies Senior Living

Financial & Wealth Advisors

When the Financial Conversation Becomes a Care Conversation

Aging and health challenges can affect a client's financial plan. When that begins to happen, we are here to provide professional insight into evolving care needs and the services and levels of support that may be required.

A change in health or independence can quickly raise questions that aren't purely financial:

  • Can my client safely remain at home?
  • What level of support may be needed?
  • What type of living environment is appropriate?
  • What might the next several years of care look like?

Where we add value

Assess

An Aging Life Care Manager from our team will evaluate current and future care needs, living environment, support system gaps, health, safety, and quality of life.

Coordinate

Our care manager will help identify, arrange, and coordinate appropriate care providers, services, and resources.

Monitor

We provide ongoing oversight as health, independence, and care needs change.

Our role: You remain the financial advisor. Our role is to provide care management expertise that helps clients and their trusted professionals better understand the care needs those financial resources may need to support.

When is the right time to recommend care management? Consider involving Caring with Grace when a client begins experiencing significant changes in health, cognition, independence, living arrangements, caregiver support, or other circumstances that may affect their existing financial plan.

Financial expertise + care management expertise = a more informed approach to aging
Trust Officers & Fiduciaries

Professional Visibility for the Clients You Protect

When you have financial or fiduciary responsibility but are not involved in a beneficiary's day-to-day life, it can be difficult to understand how circumstances are changing. Caring with Grace provides professional care management and a local presence that can bring greater visibility to the client's needs and circumstances.

Ways we can support: We provide a local presence and professional visibility into a client's needs through our processes of assessment, oversight, coordination, and communication among authorized providers, family members, and other professionals.

Legal Professionals

Care Management That Supports the Legal Plan

When aging, illness, or cognitive change creates issues beyond the legal scope, Caring with Grace helps manage the care-related complexities surrounding your client.

The intersection of legal planning and care: Legal planning for an aging or vulnerable client often raises practical questions that extend beyond legal documents. Is the client safe at home? Have cognitive changes affected daily functioning? Is the current level of care appropriate? Are family dynamics making implementation difficult? Caring with Grace helps address the care-related issues surrounding the legal plan.

How we support your work

Care Assessment

A professional perspective on the client's current circumstances, needs, support system, and care environment.

Care Coordination

We manage care-related issues and coordinate providers and resources outside the legal scope.

Changing Capacity & Function

When concerns arise, we help identify changes in function and coordinate with appropriate professionals.

Housing & Care Decisions

We help clients and their families evaluate whether the current environment remains appropriate, and coordinate next steps.

Attorney

Legal counsel and protection, estate planning and proper documents, guardianship if required.

Caring with Grace

Care assessment, coordination of care, family communication, ongoing monitoring, and crisis response.

Together

Collaboratively we assure the client is supported through their changing needs and transitions. You manage the legal complexities; we help manage the health and care complexities.

Medical Professionals

Extending the Care Plan Beyond the Exam Room

Are you concerned about what happens when the patient leaves your office? Caring with Grace helps bridge the space between medical recommendations and the realities of the patient's daily life.

A thoughtful medical plan can become difficult to implement when a patient is also dealing with cognitive change, mobility issues, transportation barriers, family dynamics, multiple providers, or an increasingly complex home environment. That is where care management can help.

Where we add value

Support Follow-Through

We help clients and families understand recommendations, coordinate next steps, and identify barriers to implementation.

Coordinate Care

We help connect physicians, specialists, home health, home care, senior living providers, and other resources.

Provide Day-to-Day Insight

We can provide perspective on circumstances that may not be visible during an office visit.

Support Families

We help families navigate non-medical issues surrounding the patient's health so the medical team can remain focused on clinical care.

Communicate Changes

When authorized, relevant changes and concerns can be communicated to the appropriate providers.

Home Care Agencies

Two Different Roles. One Client.

Caring with Grace is not a home care agency. We provide professional care management that complements the hands-on support your team provides in the home.

Home Care Agency

Hands-on direct care for the client: excellent companionship, regularly scheduled support, safety, and assistance.

Caring with Grace

Care planning, medical coordination, care transitions, addressing changing care needs, family communication.

Working Together

With a shared focus on the client, we help create greater continuity of care, more coordinated support, and clearer communication with families and other professionals.

Senior Living Professionals

Supporting Your Residents as a Trusted Partner

Senior living communities provide an essential foundation of care, safety, and connection. Caring with Grace provides an additional layer of individualized advocacy, coordination, and professional care management when a resident's situation requires more. We can bridge the gap among the resident, the family, the community, and the other professionals involved. Our goal is for the community, the resident, and the family to work collaboratively as one.

How we can be of value

  • Complex medical needs: we coordinate appointments with physicians and specialists and stay ahead of changing healthcare needs.
  • Family communication: we help keep distant or overwhelmed families updated and assist with navigating care decisions and changes.
  • Local presence: we provide a consistent professional presence for residents without nearby support.

Community

Provides the resident's daily care, support, services, safety, and community.

Caring with Grace

Provides individualized assessment, advocacy, medical coordination, and family communication.

Working Together

We collaboratively support the resident's changing needs and comfort.
