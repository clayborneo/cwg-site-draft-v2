Our Services

Caring on Call

Planning for your own aging proactively, not in crisis.

For people who like to plan ahead

A care manager in your corner, before you need one.

Most families come to us in the middle of a crisis. Caring on Call is for people who want to do it differently, by putting a professional in their corner before a health change forces tough decisions.

You're paired with a care manager and will complete the same thorough assessment every Caring with Grace client receives. If your care manager confirms your situation is stable, you move into a lighter-touch, lower-cost relationship: one planned visit a year, and a direct line to your care manager whenever you need it.

What's Included

Your Benefits

Your assigned care manager

The same professional, getting to know you and your wishes from day one, not meeting you for the first time in a crisis.

Comprehensive initial assessment and concierge care plan

An in-depth first visit covering your health, home, goals, and support network, used to confirm this level of service is right for you.

A professional green light

Your care manager reviews your assessment and confirms you are doing well enough to move to an annual rhythm. Their judgment, on record, giving you both confidence in the plan.

One planned visit a year

A yearly check-in to revisit your goals, catch small changes early, and keep your plan current.

An open line in between

Something come up between visits? Call your care manager directly. No waiting for the next scheduled check-in.

Ready to scale up support, the moment you need it

If your needs grow, your care manager already knows your history and can step up support immediately, including moving you into our standard care management relationship.

Common Questions

Caring on Call FAQ

How do I know if this is the right level of service for me?

If any of these describe you, this could be the right fit:

  • I feel fine today. Do I really need to think about this yet?
  • How do I find someone I trust before I'm in a crisis and don't have time to look?
  • What does “normal aging” look like for me, and who tells me if that changes?
  • How do I get the reassurance of a plan without paying for services I don't need yet?
Can my attorney or financial advisor be involved?

We encourage everyone to build a support network, so yes! With your permission, we're glad to coordinate with the professionals you already trust.

How much does this cost? Is it a membership?

There is no membership fee. All time with your care manager is billed at our current hourly rate, so you are only billed for the time you need.

Reach out for a no-obligation conversation about whether the service fits your situation.
