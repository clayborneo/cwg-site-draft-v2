Our Services

Care Management for Families

We provide experienced, professional guidance for older adults and families facing changing health, complex care decisions, dementia, or the challenges of aging at home.

Where to Start

What is an Aging Life Care Manager?

A Life Care Manager is a trained professional, often a nurse or social worker, who assesses your loved one's full situation, builds a personalized plan, and coordinates every moving part of their care. You gain an expert guide and a steady advocate for the road ahead.

Care management is not caregiving.

You may be familiar with caregiving, the hands-on help a family member or hired caregiver provides: meals, hygiene, transportation, companionship.

Care management is something different. Our Aging Life Care Professionals® are nurses, social workers, and health specialists who assess your full situation, build a personalized plan, and coordinate every moving part of your loved one's care.

Simply put: a caregiver provides care. A Life Care Manager makes sure you receive the right care, from the right people, at the right time.

Caregiver

Provides the care

Hands-on daily help: meals, hygiene, transportation, companionship.

Life Care Manager

Coordinates the care

Assesses needs, builds the plan, attends appointments, advocates across the system.

Is This the Right Fit?

How do I know if our services fit your situation?

If any of these sound like your family, a conversation with a Life Care Manager is a good next step.

  • A new diagnosis has your family unsure what happens next.
  • Memory changes are raising questions about safety at home.
  • Appointments, medications, and providers feel impossible to juggle.
  • You live far away and need trusted eyes and ears nearby.
  • Family members disagree about the right level of care.
  • You simply want a professional plan, before there's a crisis.

Our Process

What does the process look like?

1

Hearing the concerns

We begin with a complimentary, focused conversation about the concerns you have for your loved one and what you hope to accomplish.

2

Taking in the whole picture

We meet with your family to listen and understand the goals, health, safety, living situation, family support system, and existing resources.

3

Creating a personalized plan

Priorities are addressed in a practical plan built on solutions and resources for current and future needs.

4

Coordinating care

Decision makers agree on next steps to put the plan in motion, adding effective resources that support the senior and the family.

5

Staying involved

Your care manager actively stays involved as needed: monitoring, adjusting the plan as needs change, and communicating with everyone involved.

Why Families Call Us

Five reasons families work with a care manager

01

Expertise you don't have

Our team holds nursing, social work, and dementia certifications, plus decades of experience navigating the aging-care system.

02

One trusted point of contact

Instead of juggling doctors, home-health agencies, and facilities yourself, you have one professional coordinating it all.

03

Continuity of care

We stay involved as your loved one's needs change, through transitions, hospital stays, and new diagnoses.

04

Cost containment

The right care, at the right level, in the right setting, saving families from costly over- or under-care.

05

Peace of mind

Especially for long-distance families: a knowledgeable advocate on the ground who calls, visits, and reports back.
