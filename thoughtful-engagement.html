Our Services

Thoughtful Engagement®

Engaging activities that inspire joy. Reigniting joy for participants, and peace of mind and more time for care partners.

The Why

Joy shouldn't fade when social opportunities do.

Some people can't easily take part in day-to-day activities anymore, and some simply choose not to: a solo ager, someone who has lost a spouse, or someone living with a disease, disability, or special need. When the social world shrinks, loneliness often follows, and with it a greater risk of declining health and heavier care needs.

Thoughtful Engagement® exists to turn that around: to bring connection, fun, and purpose back into the week, one person at a time.

"Everyone deserves continued joy to sustain their sense of dignity, independence, and quality of life."

Angela Thomas, Founder & CEO
The What

A one-on-one program built around what brings each person joy

Thoughtful Engagement® is a personalized social enrichment program: real activities, chosen for one person, delivered with plenty of fun. It is the only program of its kind in Dallas, offered exclusively by Caring with Grace.

  • More than companionship. Each activity pairs a lifelong interest with a joy-producing outcome, not just time filled.
  • Built on strengths. We explore what a person can do and where they want to do it, whatever their health condition.
  • Wherever they call home. A private residence, senior community, group home, rehab center, or long-term care.
  • Purely social, always ongoing. No care tasks compete for attention. The search for new joys never stops.
How It Works

Three steps to a plan that fits like a favorite chair

1

We listen to the story

Your Specialist sits down with the participant, and with family if it helps, to uncover the interests and pastimes that have brought joy across a lifetime.

2

We build an Engagement Plan

Those life-joys become a creative, personal plan of activities and outings, matched to ability and to where the participant lives.

3

We bring it to life, together

Visit by visit, the Specialist becomes a partner in the fun, keeps exploring new possibilities, and keeps the family in the loop.

The Benefits

Joy for participants, relief for care partners

For participants

  • Renewed purpose and a fuller sense of dignity, independence, and quality of life.
  • A trusted partner for the experiences they love but can no longer do alone.
  • Activities shaped around their own strengths and interests, not a generic schedule.
  • New friendships, new discoveries, and plenty of small wins to celebrate.

For care partners

  • Peace of mind, knowing a loved one is genuinely engaged and enjoying life.
  • Time back in the week that was usually committed to a loved one's care.
  • Regular updates on well-being from another caring set of eyes.
  • Open communication and joys celebrated together, like family.
Joy in Action

A few of the joys we've uncovered

She loves gardening.

Succulent garden materials came to her room, where she calls home. Her garden grew into ongoing showings for the whole community.

He loves birding.

A bird-call identification app on his phone got him outside regularly, listening and naming every call he heard.

He was a DJ and lifelong music lover.

A visit to a vintage record store became a multi-sensory trip through the music he grew up spinning.

Meet Your Specialist

Leslie McCabe

Thoughtful Engagement® Specialist

Leslie McCabe brings warmth, patience, and a lifelong love of people to her role as a Thoughtful Engagement Specialist, where she provides personalized social enrichment for aging adults and meaningful support for their families. A Texas Christian University graduate with a degree in Social Work, Leslie spent over a decade working in her children's school—first as a teaching assistant, then in the Office of Admission—experience that sharpened her gift for listening, problem-solving, and helping others feel valued.

After losing both her father and her father-in-law, Leslie discovered a deep, natural connection with aging adults and the families walking alongside them. She loves hearing life stories, honoring the wisdom and humor of older adults, and reminding them that their experiences still matter. For families seeking dementia support or navigating the challenges of caring for aging parents, Leslie offers compassionate, personalized engagement that brings connection, confidence, and joy.
